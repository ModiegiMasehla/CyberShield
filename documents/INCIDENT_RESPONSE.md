# Incident Response Workflow

1. **Preparation** — authentication controls, audit logging, detection rules and lab data.
2. **Identification** — log analyzer and detection engine produce alerts.
3. **Containment** — analyst records containment actions in the incident.
4. **Eradication** — record root cause and remediation.
5. **Recovery** — transition the incident to RECOVERED after controls are restored.
6. **Lessons learned** — close with resolution notes and update rules or controls.
